     fixed to compare against source length instead.

     writeLang/mergePlace), rebuild (build-payload.mjs + bump-build.mjs), commit, then relaunch the
     SAME workflow script fresh (no resumeFromRunId needed) — the Scout stage always recomputes
     the true remaining set from what's actually on disk, so it naturally picks up wherever the
     last attempt left off with zero duplicate work.

ARCHITECTURE — WHAT IS NEW (this is most of what you need to learn)
THE EVIDENCE MODEL
  Safety findings live in place.safety under five keys: gf_cross_contamination, soy_sauce_wheat,
  vegan_cross_contact, staff_allergy_handling, positives. Each entry is {text, source} — or a
  bare string, which is a legacy uncited claim. A verification agent that checks a claim against
  its cited page and finds the page does not support it sets `unsupported: true`. That flag is
  the single most important bit in the dataset: it means "a human wrote this and the source does
  not say it."
  Current: ~7,900 claims, 2,558 verified, 853 repointed to a source that does support them,
  422 disproven, ~286 still uncited.

THE HUMAN GATE — and it now survives a rebuild in all three directions
  Approve → safety.owner_signoff {decision, field, from, to, by, date, reason} plus an append-only
            owner_signoff_log (a record can move on BOTH axes; one object loses the first).
  Override → owner_signoff.overrode_disproven records that Greg approved AFTER reading the
            disproven claims. Compared by COUNT, so newly-disproven claims re-assert the hold.
  Refuse  → data/_gate_rejections.json. THIS FILE EXISTS BECAUSE THE QUEUE REGENERATES. Rejections
            that lived only in the record were undone and re-applied three times (杏もん堂).
  scripts/apply-gate-tranche.mjs A|B|C [--apply] [--override=<id>,...] is the applier.
  scripts/gate-tranches.mjs A|B|C prints a tranche for review.
  THE APPROVAL UX THAT WORKED: do not hand Greg 72 individual decisions. Band by how much a wrong
  answer costs — A into a top tier (read each), B into a middle tier, C off "no" (false warnings,
  safest to bulk-approve and the most likely to matter to a hungry traveller). He approved 72
  records in three messages this way.

THE ORCHESTRATOR
  scripts/rebuild.mjs runs ~25 passes in order then lints. IT TAKES A LOCK (data/.rebuild.lock).
  A full rebuild reads every city file, works for minutes, and writes them all back — anything
  that edits data/ meanwhile LOSES. That is not hypothetical; it silently ate a verified revert
  in this session. DO NOT WRITE TO data/ WHILE A REBUILD RUNS.
  scripts/lint-data.mjs is the enforcement point and the thing you trust. It caught the one piece
  of confirmed fabrication that got through human review this session.

THE APP-SIDE TRUST WORK (Greg's directive: *"Trust is paramount for the GF/Vegan layers"*)
  Every claim in the detail panel renders with a numbered [n] citation link, or an explicit
  "uncited" marker. Hidden records are excluded from the map. SHOP_CLAIM_FALSE shows a red panel
  when a shop's own GF claim is contradicted by its own menu. A browsable 🏪 Chains modal lists
  25 chains (data/chains_kb.json) sorted by GF and by vegan.

LESSONS THAT COST REAL TIME — READ THESE BEFORE WRITING A CHECKER
1. PROSE IN A TIER FIELD, four separate times. Agents write the verdict and then explain it in
   the same field: "options — and NOT higher, on the operator's own words". Exact-enum consumers
   silently do nothing; exact-enum FILTERS make the shop invisible in the app.
   THE RULE: parse, then validate — never simply reject. Take the leading token if it is a valid
   tier and keep the sentence in a *_note field. A rejected verdict throws away the research AND
   leaves the record wrong. scripts/normalize-sweep-tiers.mjs is the reference implementation.
2. A CHECKER THAT FALSE-POSITIVES IS WORSE THAN NO CHECKER. Deleted outright: a plant-called-dairy
   checker with 34 false positives out of 36 ("uses soy milk rather than dairy" reads identically
   to the error). Narrowed hard after first drafts: vegan-contradiction (Japanese vegan menus name
   plant dishes after animal ones — 車麩の唐揚げ, 豆乳ヨーグルト, ベジツナマヨ; バターナッツかぼちゃ
   is squash; エビス beer contains エビ), self-admitted-doubt, closure detection (bare 閉店 matched
   「スープ品切れで早期閉店あります」 — closes early when the soup sells out).
3. CLASSIFY DEAD LINKS, DO NOT COUNT THEM. A first link check reported 1,086 dead; 995 were
   tabelog.com refusing a non-browser UA. 404/DNS = gone; 403/timeout = unknown. A number that
   buries 33 real failures in 1,000 fake ones is worse than no number.
4. HTTP 200 DOES NOT MEAN THE LINK IS GOOD. Four domains had lapsed and been repurposed —
   senza-x.com now serves a Korean gambling site — and every link check called them healthy.
   scripts/flag-hijacked-domains.mjs checks CONTENT, not status.
5. THE FABRICATION PATTERN, and it is subtle. 杏もん堂 was approved on "the shop's own site states
   the curry uses no wheat flour and passed a third-party gliadin test, NONE DETECTED". The site
   says nothing of the kind. The tell: the agent got the CHECKABLE part right — it corrected the
   spice count from 18 to 21, which the site does confirm — and invented the part that mattered.
   A PARTLY-VERIFIABLE FINDING IS NOT A TRUSTWORTHY ONE. Check the load-bearing claim, not the
   incidental detail that makes the finding look researched.
6. \b WRITTEN THROUGH A PYTHON HEREDOC BECOMES A 0x08 BACKSPACE BYTE and silently disables every
   word boundary in a regex. Three scripts shipped broken this way. Use node line-edits and regex
   LITERALS, not new RegExp(string). Also: apostrophes break `node -e '...'` — write patch scripts
   to a file.
7. MEASURE DRIFT AGAINST IMMUTABLE INPUTS, not against the record. The first run moves the pin, so
   every later run measures zero and the flag evaporates.
8. HARVEST, DON'T WAIT. If an agent sweep hangs on 1 of N, stop it and harvest completed records
   from the per-agent .jsonl files. (The fleet is retired, but this applies to any batch.)
9. THE GATE IS NOT SELF-ENFORCING — FOUR SEPARATE PASSES WERE SILENTLY OVERWRITING IT, and each
   one had a defensible local rule. fix-menu-promotions ("the detail text wins, it is the part
   somebody researched"). flag-divergent-duplicates ("align twins to the more cautious tier").
   enforce-cited-claims ("a disproven claim beats a sign-off"). merge-tokyo3-enrich (re-merges
   the shards from scratch every run, so it is idempotent — and therefore always the last word).
   Every one of those rules is RIGHT against another machine pass and WRONG against Greg: a
   stale duplicate, an un-rewritten paragraph, or an August shard snapshot is not new information
   about the food. The failures were invisible because the passes left no marker the linter read,
   so a decision would be made, verified, and quietly undone on the next rebuild.
   THE RULE: any pass that writes gf_confidence or vegan_status MUST check owner_signoff for that
   FIELD and data/_gate_rejections.json for that id+field before writing. Grep for tier
   assignments before trusting a pipeline run: grep -n "gf_confidence *=" scripts/*.mjs
   HOW TO FIND THE CULPRIT FAST: bisect. Set the record to the intended tier, run each rebuild
   step in order, and stop at the first one that moves it. Two of the four were found this way in
   under a minute each; reading twenty-five scripts would have taken an hour.

WHAT A FRESH CLAUDE GETS WRONG (tacit knowledge, written down 2026-08-23)
Every one of these cost real time in the session that produced this file.

A. "DISPROVEN" DOES NOT MEAN "FALSE". It means the cited page does not say this. A claim can
   be perfectly true and still flagged `unsupported` — the researcher knew the fact and cited
   the wrong page for it. The correct response is RE-SOURCE, not reverse.
   And it cuts both ways, which is the part that surprises: 山代屋 was rated "no" because "the
   anagomeshi glaze is soy-sauce based, which in Japan is TYPICALLY brewed with wheat". That
   guess was the disproven claim, so the PESSIMISTIC rating was the unsourced one. If you read
   `unsupported` as "this shop is worse than we thought", you will get the direction backwards
   on a third of the queue.

B. A MALFORMED TIER DOES NOT ERROR — THE SHOP DISAPPEARS. The app filters on exact tier
   strings, so gf_confidence: "options — and NOT higher" matches no dial and the restaurant is
   simply absent from the app. No exception, no log line, no lint failure at the time. This is
   why prose-in-a-tier-field recurred four times and why it matters far more than it looks.
   Anything that writes a tier should validate against the enum at the write site.

C. READ THE RECORD'S OWN DETAIL TEXT BEFORE TRUSTING ANY VERDICT ABOUT IT. 杏もん堂's gf_detail
   said "No gluten-free options confirmed. The signature dish is almond butter toast on wheat
   bread." The upgrade recommendation said the opposite. The record was arguing with itself and
   nobody looked. Thirty seconds of reading beats any checker.

D. THE INSTINCT TO ADD ANOTHER CHECKER IS USUALLY WRONG. The single best thing done for data
   quality in that session was DELETING a checker (plant-called-dairy, 34 false positives out of
   36). False positives do not merely waste time — they train the reader to skim the report,
   which is exactly how a real finding gets missed. A checker that cannot get under ~10% false
   positives should be a documented trap in REVIEW_PROTOCOL.md instead of a script.

E. WEAK SIGNALS LOOK STRONG WHEN YOU ARE HUNTING FABRICATION. "Queue entry has evidence: []"
   seemed like a perfect fabrication detector. Eight entries had it; SEVEN were fine, with sources
   on the record itself. What actually caught the one bad record was the linter asking a question
   about the RECORD ("a tier above ask with no cited safety evidence") rather than about the claim
   made about the record. Check the artifact, not the paperwork describing it.

F. IDs ARE NOT PARSEABLE. tokyo3__9, tokyo3_higash_55, tokyo3_oh_nana are all real and mean
   nothing structurally. Never infer city, batch, or anything else from an id. Match on the id
   or on the record, never on a substring of the id.

G. DUPLICATES EXIST AND THEY FIGHT YOU. Several shops are in the data twice with DIVERGING
   ratings (T's Kitchen, 味農家, べっぴん舎). A twin can drag a signed-off record back on every
   rebuild. Name-normalising to find them does NOT work — the twins differ as カタカナ vs romaji
   ("ティーズキッチン" vs "T's Kitchen"), which no simple normaliser unifies. A real dedupe pass
   needs address/phone/coordinates, not names.

H. ITERATION IS SLOW AND SERIALIZED. A full rebuild is 5–8 minutes, buffers all output (so it
   looks hung when it is fine), and holds data/.rebuild.lock. You cannot casually rebuild.
   BATCH every data edit, then rebuild ONCE. Editing during a rebuild loses the edit silently.
   Also looks-hung-but-is-not: build-survival.mjs is network-bound with deliberate 8s backoffs.

I. EXACT-STRING PATCHING FAILS ON THIS REPO CONSTANTLY. Between non-ASCII (ō, 「」, em dashes),
   template literals, and line-ending ambiguity, anchor-based find/replace failed roughly half the
   time here. LINE-INDEX EDITS ARE RELIABLE: read the file, findIndex on a distinctive substring,
   splice. Also: apostrophes break `node -e '...'` — write patch scripts to a file and run them.
